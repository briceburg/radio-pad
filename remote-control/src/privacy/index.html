RadioPad Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

RadioPad is an open-source remote control and audio-streaming application maintained by Brice Burgess. This policy describes the official RadioPad application and the default services at radiopad.dev.

Information RadioPad processes

  • If you choose Google Sign-In, RadioPad receives your Google account identifier, name, email address, and an ID token used to authenticate requests.
  • RadioPad stores your sign-in result and settings—such as registry, account, player, and RadioDial selections—on your device.
  • Your selected account, player, station, and playback or control commands are sent to the configured registry, switchboard, player, or stream provider as needed to perform the action.
  • Those services receive ordinary network metadata, such as your IP address and user agent, when your device connects to them.

How information is used

This information is used only to sign you in, find and control your configured players, play stations, secure the services, and diagnose failures. RadioPad does not include advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information.

Service providers and other operators

Google processes sign-in information under the Google Privacy Policy. A station’s streaming provider receives requests when you listen to its stream. If you configure another RadioPad registry or stream, that service is operated independently and its privacy practices apply.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Locally stored sign-in information remains until you sign out, clear RadioPad’s application data, or uninstall the application. Settings remain until you change them, clear the application data, or uninstall the application. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this data. The default RadioPad services validate authentication tokens without storing them as account credentials. Service operators may retain limited security and access logs.

RadioPad does not create a separate user account. Google accounts are managed through Google. Access to a RadioPad account is provisioned by its registry operator; ask that operator to remove an authorization entry associated with you.

Contact

For privacy questions about the official RadioPad application or default services, contact the maintainer through the RadioPad repository. Do not include private information in a public issue; request a private follow-up channel if needed.

Changes

Material changes will be posted here with an updated effective date. The policy source and its history are available in the RadioPad repository.
